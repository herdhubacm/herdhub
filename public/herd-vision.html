Browse bulls listed by producers on Herd Hub, with photos and contact info. Ask the seller for the bull's EPDs and check them against this list.

About these numbers: You enter the EPDs; Herd Vision does not look bulls up. An EPD predicts the average of a sire's calves, not any single calf, and it is only comparable within one breed. The gap between two sires' EPDs is the expected average gap between their calves (Oregon State Extension, How to interpret sire summaries). Low accuracy means the number can still move as calves are recorded. Angus ranks and averages are from the American Angus Association percentile breakdown, current sires, Fall 2026; checked November 2026. Look at the bull too: feet, legs, disposition and a breeding soundness exam count as much as the paper. This is a planning aid, not advice for your herd.
