About these numbers: A calf's expected EPD is the average of its sire's and dam's EPDs. Any one calf can land above or below that. EPDs are only on one scale within a breed, so calf EPDs are shown only when the bull and the cows are the same breed. The pounds comparison uses the full difference between two sires' EPDs (Oregon State Extension, How to interpret sire summaries). The hybrid vigor figure, about 3.9% on weaning weight for a first cross between British or Continental breeds, is from Cundiff and Gregory (1999) as published in Heterosis: The Only Free Lunch. Angus heifer-bull lines are from the American Angus Association percentile breakdown, current sires, Fall 2026. Checked November 2026. Planning aid only.
